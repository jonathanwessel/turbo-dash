Turbo Dash — Privacy

Last updated: November 2026

Turbo Dash is a free racing game for kids, made by a dad for his son. It has no purchases, no ads and no accounts.

What we collect

Nothing. The game does not collect, send or share any personal information, and it has no analytics, tracking or advertising.

What stays on the device

Game progress (coins, cars, ranks, the player's first name if one is typed in, and any reading or outdoor time a grown-up logs in Parent Corner) is saved only in the browser storage of the device. It never leaves the device unless a grown-up chooses to copy a save code, or turns on Google Save.

Google Save (optional, off unless a grown-up turns it on)

A grown-up can sign in with Google in Parent Corner to keep a copy of the save in their own Google Drive, in a hidden app folder only this game can read (the drive.appdata permission). The copy goes straight from the device to Google; it is not sent to us. Signing out stops it.

Children

The game is made for children and collects no information from them. Grown-up settings are behind a simple grown-up check.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/jonathanwessel/turbo-dash.
