📖 How Turbo Dash works

🏁 Racing

Steer with the ◀ ▶ buttons (or tilt the tablet, in Settings). 🚀 fires Turbo when it's full; ❓ boxes give a power-up. Driving right behind another car gives a little extra speed (💨 drafting). 🏙️ Downtown is an extra, tricky course that's always open. The 👀 chip lets him try a lap of a world that isn't open yet (no prizes). If the game feels slow on an older tablet, set ✨ Effects to Simple in Settings. Every race pays coins, plus a treasure chest. A new track opens when the one before it is won on Normal or Hard (or on Easy, if Easy is the only difficulty you allow).

⭐ Racer Rank

Ranking up needs every gauge full: 🏁 wins, 🏆 trophies (medals, cups, goals, and ⭐ mastery stars: each track gives a star at 3, 10, 25, 50 and 100 wins on a difficulty; the little bar under the stars on a track card shows how close the next one is), 🔧 garage (cars and upgrades) and 🔬 research. From Rank 10 a 🪪 Driver License gauge joins (you can turn it off below). New ranks open buildings, cups, bosses and new worlds.

🔬 Turbo Lab

Research takes real time (minutes to hours) and coins. It keeps going while the tablet is off, so there's a reason to come back tomorrow. The big one, 🧠 Auto Steer, has 100 levels and takes years. It's off in boss races and Time Trials, and you can switch it off.

🚦 Driver School & 🧮 Math Laps

A Math Lap is 5 adding or taking-away questions with pictures to count. Each lap earns a 🎟️ ticket (two with no mistakes), up to a daily limit you choose. Wrong answers just try again. The questions grow from sums to 5 up to the level you allow (adding to 10, to 10, or to 20). Tickets buy lessons that make him a better driver in every car, and lessons earn the license. Settings: Driver School → 👪 Grown-ups.

👪 Your controls (Parent Corner)

Which difficulties he may pick; a steering helper for young drivers; a break reminder (a 10-minute rest screen after the time you choose); volume and vibration; Auto Steer on/off; the License gauge; weather. Parent Corner opens by tapping the version number 5 times on the title screen, behind a grown-up question (a multiplication typed on a number pad).

💾 Saving

Emoji pictures: Twemoji by Twitter, Inc. and other contributors (CC-BY 4.0). Fonts: Racing Sans One and Nunito (SIL Open Font License). Progress saves on this tablet by itself. A save code in Parent Corner copies it to another device. Installed from the website (📲 Install on the title screen), the game opens full screen and works offline. There are no purchases, no ads and no accounts, ever.
