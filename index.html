📖 How Turbo Dash works

🏁 Racing

Steer with the ◀ ▶ buttons (or tilt the tablet, in Settings). 🚀 fires Turbo when it's full; ❓ boxes give a power-up for the round button on the left: 🛡️ a shield, 💰 8 coins, 🧲 a magnet or ⚡ a Super Turbo (a free boost that doesn't use the Turbo tank). Driving right behind another car gives a little extra speed (💨 drafting). 🏙️ Downtown is an extra, tricky course that's always open. The Preview worlds button lets him try a lap of a world that isn't open yet (no prizes). A world's own cars (the shop, cup and boss cars of 🌙 Moon Base, 🌊 Deep Blue, 🧊 Glacier Rink and 🧸 Toy Track World) are 5% faster in their own world; the Garage shows it under the car's name. If the game feels slow on an older tablet, set ✨ Effects to Simple in Settings. Every race pays coins, plus a treasure chest. A new track opens when the one before it is won on Normal or Hard (or on Easy, if Easy is the only difficulty you allow).

🏙️ Main Street

The yellow Next sign under the rank always shows one thing to do next (the next track to win, or the rank gauge that needs the most help); tap it to go there. Red "!" badges show only the two most useful things at a time: coins waiting to be collected first, then prizes to claim, then a rank-up, then something to buy or learn. The back button ◀ is at the top-left on every screen and goes back to where he came from.

🛣️ Bumps and curves

Yellow arrow signs warn of sharp curves. Bumping into something at the roadside while steering back to the road only slows the car a little; bumping while nobody is steering (or bumping again right away) is a big slowdown, so a car left alone still finishes last.

⭐ Racer Rank

Ranking up needs every gauge full: 🏁 wins, 🏆 trophies (medals, cups, goals, and ⭐ mastery stars: each track gives a star at 3, 10, 25, 50 and 100 wins on a difficulty; the little bar under the stars on a track card shows how close the next one is), 🔧 garage (cars and upgrades) and 🔬 research. New ranks open buildings, cups, bosses and new worlds.

🔬 Turbo Lab

Research takes real time (minutes to hours) and coins. It keeps going while the tablet is off, so there's a reason to come back tomorrow. The big one, 🧠 Auto Steer, has 100 levels and takes years. It's off in boss races and Time Trials, and you can switch it off.

🏦 Piqua National Bank

Coins kept in the bank earn 7% every real day (put in 100, and a day later there are 107). Coins put in more than an hour into a bank day start earning the next day, so there's no trick of adding coins just before payday. The bank keeps up to 400 coins per rank (2,000 at Rank 5), and interest is paid only on coins up to that limit. Coins in the bank can't be spent until he takes them out, which he can do any time. Changing the tablet's clock doesn't pay extra: the bank checks the time with the game's website and pays at most 3 days ahead of it until the game is online again. A good first lesson in saving.

🚦 School: 🧮 Math Laps and 📚 Word Laps

A Math Lap is 5 adding or taking-away questions with pictures to count. A Word Lap is 5 reading questions: match a picture to its word, or a word to its picture. Each lap gives a Good Student bonus: races pay +10% coins (+20% for a lap with no mistakes) for 30 real minutes. Bonuses add up, each with its own 30 minutes, up to one daily limit you choose. Nothing is permanent, so it rewards practice without making the game easier for good. Wrong answers just try again. Math grows from sums to 5 up to the level you allow; words grow from 3-letter words to look-alike words (cat, cap, can) to 4-letter and longer ones. After 20 laps he earns the School Resource Officer car. Settings: School, then Grown-ups.

👪 Your controls (Parent Corner)

Which difficulties he may pick; a steering helper for young drivers; a break reminder (a 10-minute rest screen after the time you choose); volume and vibration; Auto Steer on/off; weather. Parent Corner opens by tapping the version number 5 times on the title screen, behind a grown-up question (a multiplication typed on a number pad).
