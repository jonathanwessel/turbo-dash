**Full description** (4000 max)
Turbo Dash is a bright, free racing game made by a dad for his six-year-old, and now for every kid who loves fast
toy cars.

🏁 RACE, WIN, UPGRADE
Win races to earn coins, then spend them on faster cars, engines, turbo, tires, paint and stickers. Every win opens
something new: tracks, cups, bosses and whole new worlds.

🌍 A HUGE ADVENTURE
Start on Main Street in Piqua, Ohio, and race through sunny hills, beaches, farms, pirate ports, deserts, snowy
peaks, candy land and lava mountains, then blast off to the Moon, dive under the sea, slide across a glacier, and
finally shrink down into Toy Track World: real 3D plastic tracks with loop-the-loops and booster stations.

🔬 SOMETHING TO COME BACK FOR
Research upgrades in the Turbo Lab, open a daily gift, finish daily missions, grow the town as you rank up, wash your
muddy car at the Car Wash, and fill the Museum with your trophies.

🧮 MATH SCHOOL
Math Laps with pictures to count: simple adding and taking away that grows with your child. Right answers earn Driver School
tickets that make your child a better driver in the game.

👪 MADE FOR KIDS, SET UP BY PARENTS
• No ads. No in-app purchases. No accounts. Nothing is collected.
• Pick which difficulties your child can choose, add a gentle steering helper, set a break reminder.
• Big buttons and pictures, made for little hands. Tilt-to-steer optional.
• Works offline once installed.
